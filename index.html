Bozhao Li 李博钊

I am a master's student in Cyberspace Security at Beihang University. Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer and Information Science from Southwest University.

My interests are in robot learning, control, and autonomous systems. I work with legged robots, manipulators, and aerial robots, and I am interested in how dynamics, sensing, and control shape the behavior of learned policies.

Recently, I have been working on whole-body control for a quadruped with an arm, visual landing with an actuated gimbal, and scene reconstruction for simulation. This page collects some of my research and engineering work.

01 / B2ARX · robot learning and deployment

Whole-body control and simulation for B2ARX

Research project · source not public

I study whole-body control with a Unitree B2 and an ARX R5A arm. The reaching policies combine PPO, teacher–student learning, and observation history, with a separate MuJoCo runtime for deployment. I also use this platform to explore ski-and-pole locomotion, combining parametric equipment models with rigid-body and MPM snow simulation.

我在 Unitree B2 与 ARX R5A 上研究全身控制，结合 PPO、教师—学生学习和历史观测训练到达策略，并维护独立的 MuJoCo 部署工程。同时基于这一平台探索双雪板与单杖运动，将参数化器材建模、刚体接触和 MPM 雪地仿真结合起来。

Methods & tools. Isaac Lab, Newton/MJWarp, PyTorch, and RSL-RL for learning; identified arm dynamics and MuJoCo / TorchScript / ONNX Runtime for deployment; CadQuery, OpenUSD, and MPM for equipment and snow simulation.

The skiing clip shows a simulation prototype; sustained pole-driven motion is still under development.

02 / ARX R5A · planning, contact, and camera observations

Contact-rich manipulation and demonstration generation

Simulation & data ↗ ROS 2 / cuMotion ↗ Perception & manipulation ↗

I developed an ARX R5A door-opening environment that combines cuMotion planning with PhysX contact simulation. It models the handle, latch, and door panel, and records synchronized overview and wrist-camera observations. The demonstrations cover keeping hold of the knob and transferring the grasp to the door edge, with variations in door pose and appearance.

我为 ARX R5A 搭建了开门操作仿真环境，结合 cuMotion 规划和 PhysX 接触仿真，建模把手、门锁与门板，并同步记录全景和腕部相机。示范覆盖持续抓旋钮、换抓门边两种策略，以及门的摆位和外观变化。

Methods & tools. Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab, PhysX, cuMotion, SkillGen, and HDF5 for simulated demonstrations; ROS 2, MoveIt 2, and ros2_control for planning and execution interfaces.

These are physics-based demonstrations for data generation. GR00T policy training is a separate next step.

04 / DingDong / QRLand · aerial robot learning

Visual landing with an actuated gimbal

Research project · source not public

I am exploring how a quadrotor and its gimbal can learn to land together when visual measurements are delayed or the target leaves the camera view. The simulator combines measured aircraft parameters, an identified flight-control model, articulated camera joints, and touchdown contact. I use it to study the interaction between flight control, gaze direction, and landing behavior.

我在研究无人机与云台如何协同完成视觉降落，关注量测延迟、目标离开视野和接地阶段。仿真结合实测机体参数、辨识得到的飞控模型、可动相机关节与接触动力学，用来分析机体控制、视线方向和降落行为之间的关系。

Methods & tools. Isaac Lab, Newton/MJWarp, PyTorch, PPO/skrl, PX4-style control loops, system identification, and camera geometry.

The landing clip is a static-platform simulation replay with delayed geometric detections. The four-task video shows earlier flight-control experiments.

05 / Geometry, Gaussian reconstruction, and simulation

Scene reconstruction from RGB-D and phone video

Research project · source not public

I work on reconstructing indoor scenes from RealSense recordings and phone video. My pipeline covers camera poses, depth fusion, Gaussian reconstruction, and NuRec USDZ export for viewing in Isaac Sim. I compare recorded views with rendered results, while treating geometric reconstruction and appearance modeling as separate parts of the problem.

我也在做 RealSense 录像与手机视频的室内场景重建，涵盖相机位姿、深度融合、高斯重建和 NuRec USDZ 导出，并在 Isaac Sim 中检查渲染。工作中分别检查几何与外观，对照真实拍摄画面评估结果。

Methods & tools. D455 RGB-D, cuSFM, PyCOLMAP and bundle adjustment, nvblox TSDF, NVIDIA 3DGRUT / 3DGUT, NuRec USDZ, and Isaac Sim.

D455 and iPhone results shown here come from separate captures and reconstructions. The monocular iPhone reconstruction has arbitrary scale.

06 / ARX R5 · real-to-sim modeling

Identifying actuator dynamics from measured motion

Code ↗

I identify simulation-equivalent actuator dynamics from ARX joint recordings. The model fits command delay, PD gains, friction, and target bias, then tests the result on held-out trajectories. A differentiable PyTorch model and Newton/MuJoCo replay let me compare fitted dynamics with measured motion and inspect where the model still falls short.

我从 ARX 关节记录中辨识仿真等效的执行器动态，拟合命令延迟、PD 参数、摩擦和目标偏置，再用留出轨迹检验。结合 PyTorch 可微模型与 Newton/MuJoCo 回放，对比实测运动并定位模型偏差。

Methods & tools. C++ and the ARX SDK/CAN interface for collection; Python, PyTorch, grey-box optimization, Newton, MuJoCo, and Warp for identification and replay.

07 / PX4 · excitation, logging, and frequency response

Frequency-sweep experiments for aircraft identification

Code ↗

I implemented a configurable frequency-sweep mode using the official PX4 ROS 2 interface. It supports linear and logarithmic chirps, reference holding, telemetry checks, and experiment logging. I use flight logs to examine gain, phase, and coherence before fitting the dynamics used by the simulator.

我基于官方 PX4 ROS 2 接口实现可配置扫频模式，支持线性和对数扫频、参考位置保持、遥测检查与实验记录，再从飞行日志分析幅值、相位和相干性，为仿真中的动力学辨识提供依据。

Methods & tools. C++, ROS 2, px4_ros2_interface_lib, trajectory setpoints, ULog, chirp excitation, and frequency-response analysis.

08 / Articulated mechanisms and contact simulation

From a CAD assembly to a simulated docking station

Research project · source not public

I turned a complete landing-station CAD assembly into a USD asset while preserving its geometry and mechanical structure. The simulation adds rigid bodies, collision shapes, and a closed-loop linkage: one driven rotor moves four passive centering bars. I use recorded PhysX states to inspect the closing motion and contact with a freely moving drone.

我把完整停机平台 CAD 装配整理成 USD 资产，保留原始几何和机械结构，补充刚体、碰撞体与闭环连杆。一个驱动转子带动四路被动对中横杠，通过 PhysX 状态回放观察收拢过程及其与自由运动无人机的接触。

Methods & tools. STEP, OpenUSD, Python, UsdPhysics, closed-loop articulation, convex collision geometry, and Isaac Sim / PhysX.

01

Odometry-Guided Command-Space Drift Correction for a Frozen RL Quadruped-Arm Policy

Abstract

Mounted manipulators can shift the center of mass of a quadruped and turn a zero lateral-velocity command into meter-scale corridor drift. This paper investigates this failure mode on a Unitree B2 carrying an ARX R5a arm, and further proposes a command-space correction layer that adopts FAST-LIVO2 pose estimates to adjust only the shared velocity command of a frozen, already-trained reinforcement-learning policy. The correction is policy-agnostic, i.e., the policy layer and low-level joint control remain unchanged. On real hardware, the corrector reduces the right-extreme drift from 5.18 m to 0.15 m over a 25 m corridor, a 97% reduction to the decimeter level, without retraining or an explicit CoM model.

04

IRA: Interception-Risk-Aware Trajectory Planning for MAV Evasion from Multiple Pursuers in Cluttered Environments

Abstract

This letter investigates trajectory planning for an autonomous micro air vehicle (MAV) evading multiple pursuers in cluttered environments. The evader MAV aims to reach a goal region while avoiding both obstacles and capture by pursuers. Unlike existing approaches that rely on predicting pursuers' future trajectories, the proposed planner explicitly incorporates interception-risk assessment into path planning and trajectory optimization, requiring only the pursuers' current positions and maximum speeds. This design is inspired by geometric methods for solving reach-avoid games, and it enables safe navigation without forecasting pursuer motions. To address situations where the goal region becomes unreachable due to complete coverage by interception-risk cells, a nearest safely reachable point (NSRP) is also introduced to terminate path search early and improve computational efficiency. The proposed approach is evaluated through five groups of simulations with different speed settings. Each group contains 100 instances with randomly generated initial positions. Compared with a baseline planner that neglects interception risk, the proposed planner significantly improves the success rate for the evader MAV to reach the goal region without being intercepted across all simulations. Real-world experiments with four MAVs further validate the proposed approach.
